Speed Dome Cameras

  • The speed Dome Cameras are high Megapixel, wide dynamic range, surveillance solution capable of streaming beyond 1080p high definition footage.
  • This camera features high image resolution, with 100m infrared range for low light/night video capture and optical zoom.
  • Speed Dome camera supports internal SD card storage and can store footage locally, providing a truly standalone surveillance system that doesn’t require a connection to a central storage device.
